A Solo is like a group, but only one of its children is visible at a time, called the active child. Use it to switch between versions of something, such as hats on a character, views of a rig, or frames of an animation.

Switching a Solo is cheaper than animating the opacity of each object. The inactive children aren't drawn or updated at all.

<YouTube id="diYNK8aidaE" />

## Creating a Solo

- **From a selection**: Select objects in the Hierarchy, right-click, and choose **Wrap in** > **Solo**.
- **From a group**: Right-click a group and choose **Convert to Solo**.
- **With the Solo tool**: Select the **Solo** tool from the **Arrangement Tools** menu in the toolbar (or press `S`), click the stage, then drag objects into the Solo in the Hierarchy.

![Wrapping selected objects in a Solo from the Hierarchy's right-click menu](/images/editor/manipulating-shapes/wrap-in-solo.webp)

## Choosing the active child

Click the radio button next to a child in the Hierarchy, or choose it from **Active** in the Inspector.

Some children aren't options and are always applied, such as constraints and clipping shapes.

## Animating a Solo

In a timeline, click a child's radio button to key the active child. The Solo switches instantly at each key.

![A Solo with three hat options in the Hierarchy, and keys on the Solo's row in the timeline](/images/editor/manipulating-shapes/animate-solo.webp)

To switch the active child from your app, [bind](/editor/data-binding/overview) the Solo's **Active** property. See [Controlling Solos with enums](/editor/data-binding/enums#controlling-solos-with-enums).

## Examples

### Skins

Put each version of a part in its own Solo to build swappable skins.

![Switching a car's air intake and stickers by clicking the radio buttons of two Solos](/images/editor/manipulating-shapes/solo-skins.webp)

### Switching rigs

Rig a character from several angles, put each rig in a Solo, and switch between them as the character turns.

![Switching a character between a front view rig and a 3/4 view rig with a Solo](/images/editor/manipulating-shapes/solo-switch-rigs.gif)

### Frame-by-frame animation

Put each frame in a Solo and key the active child on each frame of the timeline.

![Switching between mouth images on a dragon character with a Solo](/images/editor/manipulating-shapes/solo-frame-by-frame.gif)
