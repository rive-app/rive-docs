---
title: 'Trim and Dash'
description: 'Draw only part of a path with a trim, or break it into dashes and gaps, and animate either one.'
---

Trim and Dash are path effects. They change which parts of a path a stroke or fill draws, without changing the shape itself. They're most often used on strokes:

- **Trim** draws only part of the path. Animate it to make a line draw on, draw off, or travel along a path.
- **Dash** breaks the path into a repeating pattern of dashes and gaps. Animate its offset to make the dashes march along the path.

## Adding a trim or dash

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Select the shape, then click the options button on the stroke or fill in the **Fills and Strokes** section of the Inspector.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Open the **Effects** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    In **Path Effects**, click **+** and select **Trim Effect** or **Dash Effect**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each stroke and fill has its own effects, and you can stack more than one. To share the same trim or dash across many shapes, put it in an [Effect Group](/editor/manipulating-shapes/effect-groups).

## Trim

### Start and end

**Start** and **End** set which part of the path is drawn, as a percentage of the path's length. Only the section between them is visible. Key them on a timeline to animate the trim, for example from an **End** of 0% to 100% to draw a line on.

![Changing a trim's Start and End](/images/editor/trim-and-dash/trim-start-end.gif)

### Offset

**Offset** moves the visible section along the path, keeping its length the same. Animate it to make a segment travel around a shape.

![Changing a trim's Offset to move the visible section along the path](/images/editor/trim-and-dash/trim-offset.gif)

### Sequential and Synced

When a shape has more than one path, the trim's mode decides how they're trimmed:

- **Sequential** treats the paths as one long path, trimming them one after another in their order under the shape.
- **Synced** trims every path at the same time, by the same percentage.

![A Sequential trim drawing paths one after another](/images/editor/trim-and-dash/trim-sequential.gif)

![A Synced trim drawing all paths at the same time](/images/editor/trim-and-dash/trim-synced.gif)

Set the mode to **None** to remove the trim.

## Dash

![A dashed stroke around a rounded rectangle](/images/editor/trim-and-dash/dash-example.gif)

### Dashes and gaps

A dash starts with one **Dash** length. Click **Add gap** to add a gap after it, then **Add dash** to add another dash, building up a pattern that repeats along the path. Each length can be in pixels or a percentage of the path's length.

![Setting a dash length and adding a gap](/images/editor/trim-and-dash/dash-length.gif)

### Offset

**Offset** moves the dash pattern along the path, in pixels or a percentage. Animate it to make the dashes travel along the path.

![Changing a dash's Offset to move the dashes along the path](/images/editor/trim-and-dash/dash-offset.gif)
