Fills and strokes are the paint on a [shape](/editor/fundamentals/shapes-and-paths-overview). A fill colors the area inside the shape's paths, and a stroke draws a line along them. A shape can have any number of fills and strokes, and each one applies to every path in the shape.

Fills and strokes are listed in the **Fills and Strokes** section of the Inspector. On a [Layout](/editor/layouts/layouts-overview), the same section is called **Background**, or **Foreground** for a Layout's foreground.

## Adding a fill or stroke

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Select a shape.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    In the **Fills and Strokes** section of the Inspector, click **+** and select **Fill** or **Stroke**.

    ![Adding a fill with the + button in the Fills and Strokes section](/images/editor/fill-and-stroke/add-fill.gif)
  </Step>
</Steps>

A fill's swatch is a filled square, and a stroke's swatch is an outlined square. A new fill on a shape starts as a gradient, and a new stroke starts as a solid color.

![Adding a stroke, shown with an outlined swatch in the Fills and Strokes section](/images/editor/fill-and-stroke/add-stroke.gif)

To change a fill or stroke's color or switch it to a gradient, click its swatch. See [Color and Gradients](/editor/fundamentals/color-and-gradients).

## Managing fills and strokes

- **Reorder**: Drag a fill or stroke by an empty area of its row. Items higher in the list draw in front of items lower in the list.
- **Hide**: Click the eye icon. The fill or stroke stays on the shape, but doesn't draw.
- **Delete**: Click the minus icon.
- **Duplicate, copy, or convert**: Right-click a fill or stroke to duplicate or copy it, or to convert a fill to a stroke and a stroke to a fill.

![Reordering fills by dragging them in the Fills and Strokes section](/images/editor/fill-and-stroke/reorder-fills.gif)

![Hiding a fill with the eye icon and deleting it with the minus icon](/images/editor/fill-and-stroke/hide-delete-fill.gif)

## Fill options

Click the options button on a fill to open its options.

![Fill Options showing Name, Blend, Fill Rule, and Feather](/images/editor/fill-and-stroke/fill-options.png)

- **Name**: The fill's name.
- **Blend**: The fill's [blend mode](#blend-modes).
- **Fill Rule**: How overlapping paths are filled. See [Fill rules](#fill-rules).
- **Feather**: Softens the fill's edge. See [Vector Feathering](/editor/fundamentals/vector-feathering).

The **Effects** tab adds path effects, such as a trim or dash, to the fill. See [Path effects](#path-effects).

### Fill rules

When a shape has more than one path, or a path crosses itself, the fill rule decides which areas are inside the shape and get filled. To cut a hole in a shape, put the hole's path inside the shape's outer path and choose a fill rule that leaves the overlap empty.

- **Non-zero** is the default. Each path counts +1 if it's drawn clockwise and −1 if it's drawn counter-clockwise. Areas where the total isn't zero are filled. Two overlapping paths drawn in the same direction fill the overlap, and two drawn in opposite directions leave it empty.
- **Even-odd** ignores direction and counts how many paths surround an area. Areas inside an odd number of paths are filled, and areas inside an even number are empty. A path inside another path always makes a hole.
- **Clockwise** is a fill rule made by Rive. Paths drawn clockwise fill, and paths drawn counter-clockwise cut away from them, so you can subtract one path from another by reversing its direction. Feathered fills always use Clockwise.

To change a path's direction, use **Reverse Direction** while [editing its vertices](/editor/fundamentals/edit-vertices).

## Stroke options

Set a stroke's thickness in the field on its row. Click the options button on a stroke to open the rest of its options.

- **Name**: The stroke's name.
- **Blend**: The stroke's [blend mode](#blend-modes).
- **Cap**: How the ends of an open path look.
  - **Butt** ends the stroke flat at the end point. A path with no length doesn't draw.
  - **Round** adds a rounded end. A path with no length draws a circle.
  - **Square** adds a square end that extends past the end point. A path with no length draws a square.
- **Join**: How corners look: **Round**, **Bevel**, or **Miter**.
- **Apply transform**: When it's on, scaling the shape also scales the stroke's thickness. Turn it off to keep the thickness the same at any scale.
- **Feather**: Softens the stroke's edge. See [Vector Feathering](/editor/fundamentals/vector-feathering).

The **Effects** tab adds path effects to the stroke. See [Path effects](#path-effects).

## Blend modes

Each fill and stroke has its own blend mode. By default it's set to inherit the shape's blend mode.

## Path effects

Path effects change the path a fill or stroke draws, without changing the shape itself. Add them from the **Effects** tab of a fill or stroke's options:

- **Trim Effect** draws only part of the path, so you can animate a line drawing on. See [Trim and Dash](/editor/manipulating-shapes/trim-path).
- **Dash Effect** breaks the path into dashes and gaps. See [Trim and Dash](/editor/manipulating-shapes/trim-path#dash).
- **Effect Group** applies a shared stack of effects that you control in one place. See [Effect Groups](/editor/manipulating-shapes/effect-groups).
- **Scripted path effects** apply a [path effect script](/scripting/protocols/path-effect-scripts).

## Vector Feathering

To soften the edge of a fill or stroke, for glows, drop shadows, and inner shadows, use [Vector Feathering](/editor/fundamentals/vector-feathering).

## Effect Groups

To share the same trim, dash, or scripted effects across many fills and strokes, and control them from one place, use an [Effect Group](/editor/manipulating-shapes/effect-groups).
