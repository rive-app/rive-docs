---
title: TextureFormat
---

Pixel format of a texture or color target. The block compressed formats depend on
the device; check [GPUFeatures](/scripting/animascript/api-reference/gpu/gpu-features) before using them.


## Fields

### `r8unorm`

One 8 bit normalized channel.


### `rg8unorm`

Two 8 bit normalized channels.


### `rgba8unorm`

Four 8 bit normalized channels.


### `rgba8snorm`

Four 8 bit signed normalized channels.


### `bgra8unorm`

Four 8 bit normalized channels in BGRA order.


### `rgba16float`

Four 16 bit float channels. Rendering into it needs the `colorBufferHalfFloat` feature.


### `rg16float`

Two 16 bit float channels. Rendering into it needs the `colorBufferHalfFloat` feature.


### `r16float`

One 16 bit float channel. Rendering into it needs the `colorBufferHalfFloat` feature.


### `rgba32float`

Four 32 bit float channels. Rendering into it needs the `colorBufferFloat` feature.


### `rg32float`

Two 32 bit float channels. Rendering into it needs the `colorBufferFloat` feature.


### `r32float`

One 32 bit float channel. Rendering into it needs the `colorBufferFloat` feature.


### `rgb10a2unorm`

10 bit RGB and 2 bit alpha, normalized.


### `r11g11b10float`

Packed 11, 11 and 10 bit float RGB with no alpha.


### `depth16unorm`

16 bit normalized depth.


### `depth24plusStencil8`

At least 24 bits of depth plus 8 bits of stencil.


### `depth32float`

32 bit float depth.


### `depth32floatStencil8`

32 bit float depth plus 8 bits of stencil.


### `bc1unorm`

BC1 (DXT1) compressed RGBA. Needs the `bc` feature.


### `bc3unorm`

BC3 (DXT5) compressed RGBA. Needs the `bc` feature.


### `bc7unorm`

BC7 compressed RGBA. Needs the `bc` feature.


### `etc2rgb8`

ETC2 compressed RGB. Needs the `etc2` feature.


### `etc2rgba8`

ETC2 compressed RGBA. Needs the `etc2` feature.


### `astc4x4`

ASTC compressed with 4x4 blocks. Needs the `astc` feature.


### `astc6x6`

ASTC compressed with 6x6 blocks. Needs the `astc` feature.


### `astc8x8`

ASTC compressed with 8x8 blocks. Needs the `astc` feature.
