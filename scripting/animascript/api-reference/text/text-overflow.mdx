---
title: TextOverflow
---

What a [Text](/scripting/animascript/api-reference/text/text) does with text that does not fit its box.


## Fields

### `visible`

Overflowing text still draws.


### `hidden`

Lines that do not fit are hidden.


### `clipped`

Text is clipped to the box.


### `ellipsis`

The last line that fits ends with an ellipsis.
