---
title: TextWordBreak
---

How a word that does not fit its line may be split.


## Fields

### `breakWord`

Moves the word to its own line and only cuts it when it still does
not fit there.


### `normal`

Never cuts a word; one that does not fit even on a line of its own
overflows the box.


### `breakAll`

Cuts at any character so every line is packed to the edge.
