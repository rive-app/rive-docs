---
title: TextAlign
---

Horizontal alignment of each line in a [Text](/scripting/animascript/api-reference/text/text).


## Fields

### `left`

Aligns lines to the left edge.


### `right`

Aligns lines to the right edge.


### `center`

Centers lines.


### `start`

Aligns lines to the edge where the first paragraph starts, so right
to left text starts on the right.


### `end`

Aligns lines to the edge where the first paragraph ends.
