---
title: BlendMode
---

How a paint's color or gradient is composited with the content behind it.
```ts
let paint = new Paint();
paint.color = Color.rgb(255, 100, 50);
paint.blendMode = BlendMode.multiply;
```


## Fields

### `srcOver`

Draws over the content behind it. The default.


### `additive`

Adds the colors, which lightens.


### `screen`

Multiplies the inverted colors and inverts the result, which lightens.


### `overlay`

Multiplies or screens depending on the color behind.


### `darken`

Keeps the darker of the two colors.


### `lighten`

Keeps the lighter of the two colors.


### `colorDodge`

Brightens the color behind to reflect the paint.


### `colorBurn`

Darkens the color behind to reflect the paint.


### `hardLight`

Multiplies or screens depending on the paint color.


### `softLight`

Darkens or lightens depending on the paint color, softer than `hardLight`.


### `difference`

Subtracts the darker color from the lighter one.


### `exclusion`

Like `difference` with lower contrast.


### `multiply`

Multiplies the colors, which darkens.


### `hue`

The hue of the paint with the saturation and luminosity behind.


### `saturation`

The saturation of the paint with the hue and luminosity behind.


### `color`

The hue and saturation of the paint with the luminosity behind.


### `luminosity`

The luminosity of the paint with the hue and saturation behind.
